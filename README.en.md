# Bio-Style Martial Arts · Player Feedback

Share feedback on the Bio-Style Martial Arts series for Project Zomboid, including combat moves, dodges, hit reactions, and martial arts styles.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/rgayuurp-del/BioStyle-Feedback/issues/new?template=bug-en.yml) · Tell us what you did and what happened in-game.
- [Suggest a gameplay improvement](https://github.com/rgayuurp-del/BioStyle-Feedback/issues/new?template=idea-en.yml) · Tell us what you would like to change about moves or controls.
- [Browse existing feedback](https://github.com/rgayuurp-del/BioStyle-Feedback/issues) · If someone already reported it, add your weapon, input sequence, or retest results to that issue.

Screenshots, short clips, and relevant log excerpts are optional. Reports are public, so please remove private information such as account details and server addresses. You can also leave a comment on the Steam Workshop.
